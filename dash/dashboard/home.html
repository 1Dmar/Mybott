Before you connect

Useful answers, without the fine print fog.

How do I get started?

Use Invite ProMcBot to add the bot to your Discord community, then open the Dashboard to continue configuration.

Which permissions are required?

The required permissions depend on the Discord tools you enable. Review the authorization screen and configure only what your community workflow needs.

Does it work with my Minecraft server?

Plugin and server compatibility is documented in the project’s compatibility materials. Check the current documentation before connecting a production server.

How many servers can I connect?

Access and limits can depend on the plan and current entitlement. Sign in to the Dashboard to see the options available to your account.

What happens when telemetry is missing?

Public stats keep the value unmeasured rather than inferring it. A server owner can choose whether to share the resulting aggregate card.

Where can I find support and terms?

Use the Documentation link for setup guidance, and review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in the footer for the current product boundaries.
