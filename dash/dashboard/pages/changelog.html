v1.6.0October 23, 2026
NEWIMPROVEDSECURITY

Your Minecraft identity, ready to share

Player accounts now connect Discord and Minecraft into one privacy-first identity that is useful in the dashboard and inside Discord.

What’s new

  • Global Player Account with verified Minecraft identities and server-scoped measurements.
  • Shareable /playercard command with global or current-server views and optional cards for other users.
  • Measured player cards show real joins and play time instead of invented statistics.
  • Player Center now supports templates, bio, privacy, and public card visibility.

Reliability and security

  • Legacy player links migrate automatically into the new account model.
  • Public cards never expose another user’s server-scoped activity.
  • One-time link claims remain expiring, signed, and tenant-scoped.
