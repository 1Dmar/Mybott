Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ProMcBot processes information when you use the ProMcBot Discord bot, Dashboard, public profile pages, or Minecraft plugin.

Effective date: September 1, 2026Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Scope

ProMcBot is a software service that connects Discord server management, a web Dashboard, public profile features, Minecraft server telemetry, and optional automation and billing features. This policy applies to information processed through those services and does not govern Discord, Mojang/Microsoft, PayPal, or any other third-party service itself.

Important: ProMcBot is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord, Mojang AB, Microsoft, or PayPal.

2. Information we process

We process only the information needed to authenticate accounts, provide requested features, secure the service, and operate the Dashboard.

3. Discord OAuth and permissions

When you choose Discord login, Discord authenticates you and returns the permissions you authorize. ProMcBot requests identity, server-list, and email scopes only where configured for the Dashboard. ProMcBot does not receive your Discord password.

Server access is checked against the Discord server information available to the authenticated account. ProMcBot uses that information to show manageable workspaces and does not treat an ordinary website parameter as proof of server ownership.

4. Minecraft plugin and telemetry

The optional Minecraft plugin sends signed requests to ProMcBot when configured by a server owner. The service uses plugin credentials, timestamps, nonces, and signatures to authenticate those requests. Plugin signing secrets are stored encrypted where retained and are not intended to be displayed again after provisioning.

Telemetry is used to produce server-scoped operational views. ProMcBot does not intentionally publish player names, Discord member lists, raw event streams, or private server data through public statistics pages.

5. How information is used

  • To authenticate users and maintain Dashboard sessions.
  • To verify access to Discord server workspaces and provide requested bot, plugin, analytics, automation, and profile features.
  • To protect the service against fraud, abuse, replayed plugin requests, unauthorized access, and excessive traffic.
  • To process optional subscriptions and communicate service or security information.
  • To diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve features using aggregated or operational information.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use private server telemetry to create public player profiles or publish raw player activity.

6. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by providers required to operate the service, including Discord for OAuth and Discord APIs, hosting and database providers, content delivery or infrastructure providers, and PayPal when a payment is initiated. Providers receive only information needed for their role or information you direct them to receive.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights and security of users or the service, to investigate abuse, or in connection with a lawful change in service ownership.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the relevant feature, maintain security and audit records, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Retention periods can differ by category and provider. Expiring telemetry, notifications, sessions, and operational records may be removed according to their configured retention rules.

We use access controls, authentication checks, encrypted storage for selected plugin secrets, signed plugin requests, rate limits, and least-privilege responses. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; please protect your Discord account and never share plugin credentials.

8. Your choices and rights

You may stop using the service, revoke ProMcBot’s Discord authorization through Discord, remove or change optional public profile information from the Dashboard, and ask us to review or delete information where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention reasons.

9. Children

ProMcBot is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use Discord and the applicable law. Do not use the service if you are not permitted to use Discord or the relevant service in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review it.
