Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026  • 

1. Introduction

ProMcBot ("we", "us") provides a Discord bot and web dashboard for managing Minecraft server communities. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

2. What We Collect

  • Discord account data: when you log in via Discord OAuth, we store your Discord ID, username, and avatar to operate your dashboard session.
  • Guild data: server ID, configured settings (welcome messages, modules, auto-responders, tickets, log channels) so the bot can apply them.
  • Minecraft server data: IPs/ports you enter are used only to display Minecraft server status and player information inside your dashboard.
  • Internal notifications & audit logs: events recorded by the dashboard and bot are kept in our database for your server's management history.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the bot features you enable and to provide the dashboard. We do not sell, share, or publish your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Cookies & Sessions

The dashboard uses encrypted sessions to keep you signed in. No third-party tracking cookies are required for the core experience.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your server's stored settings at any time from the in your server settings page. When a server removes the bot, we clean up related records.

6. Security

Dashboard traffic is served over HTTPS, sessions are encrypted, and protected routes require Discord authentication. Bot tokens and secrets are kept on the server and are never exposed to the client.

7. Children

ProMcBot follows Discord's Terms of Service, which require users to be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the official ProMcBot support server.
