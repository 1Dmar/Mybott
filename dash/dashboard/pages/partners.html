A thoughtful partnership

Partner Requirements

Simple expectations. Genuine communities. Better partnerships.

Active Community

Your community should have real activity and an engaged Minecraft, gaming, or Discord audience.

Genuine Members

We value genuine communities and meaningful engagement more than raw member counts.

Active Discord

Partners should have an active Discord community where ProMcBot can be introduced naturally.

Genuine Promotion

Partners should be willing to introduce ProMcBot through reasonable and agreed placements, such as a Discord announcement, resource channel, website, or other relevant community spaces.

Community Quality

We look for communities that provide a safe, respectful, and legitimate experience for their members.

Long-Term Collaboration

We are looking for genuine partnerships that can grow over time, not partnerships created only to receive free benefits.

Manual Approval

Every partnership is reviewed manually by the ProMcBot team to make sure it is a good fit.

What we expect

We don't expect partners to spam their community or promise unrealistic results. We simply ask partners to give ProMcBot genuine visibility when appropriate and work with us to build a useful long-term relationship.

Why this matters

Partners help introduce ProMcBot to new Minecraft communities, while ProMcBot provides useful tools, partner benefits, support, and opportunities to grow together.
